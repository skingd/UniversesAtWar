# Battle Amor Detachment Configuration
This document specifies how Battle Armor detachments should work.

`Battle Armor Heat Threshold` Battle Armor OMITS `Heat Threshold`. No heat mechanic exists in their weapons or their profile.

## Detachment Size
All Battle Armor detachments being with a certain number of models and may be upgraded to take additional models for a point cost.


### Clan Battle Armor

`Base Detachment Size`: 5 squads in a `Clan Star`
`Detachment Size Upgrade Option`: up to 5 additional squads

### Inner Sphere Battle Armor

`Base Detachment Size`: 4 squads in an `Inner Sphere Company`
`Detachment Size Upgrade Option`: up to 4 additional squads

## Battle Armor Saving Throw
All battle armor has a 5+ save

## Battle Armor Special Rules
All Battle Armor has the Skimmer special rule

## battle Armor Points
Battle Armor `Clan Stars` and `Inner Sphere Companies` are 10% of their BV 2.0 values in point cost.

## Battle Armor CAF
Battle Armor WITH a `Claw` or any other manipulator or melee type weapon(sword, spear, flail, etc) EXCEPT the `mine removal tool` have a CAF of +4

Battle Armor WITHOUT a `Claw` or any other manipulator or melee weapon (sword, spear, flail, etc), INCLUDING the `mine removal tool` have a CAF of +1
